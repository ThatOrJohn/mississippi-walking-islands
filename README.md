# Mississippi's Walking Islands

An animated chart of Mississippi's barrier islands (Cat, Ship, Horn, Petit Bois) and Dauphin Island, Alabama, from the first U.S. Coast Survey maps of 1848 to Landsat imagery in 2025.

**Live:** https://thatorjohn.github.io/mississippi-walking-islands/

- Island outlines are real shorelines: historical surveys (1848–1986) digitized by the Mississippi Office of Geology, and USGS Landsat shoreline products (1984–2025).
- Hurricane tracks and intensities come from NOAA's HURDAT2 best-track record and are drawn to scale. The hurricane-force wind radius comes from the record from 2004 on and is estimated from intensity before that (Willoughby & Rahn, 2004). Cloud patterns are illustrative.
- Look closely: period vessels and wildlife appear in the years they belong. Hover or tap them for the story and source.

The whole experience is a single self-contained `index.html`. Sources are listed at the bottom of the page.
